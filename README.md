# Dev-of-an-AI-Powered-Resume-Analy-and-Job-Recom-Sys-with-Autom-LinkedIn-Data-Ext_Feb_Batch-8_2026
An intelligent career development platform that analyzes resumes, evaluates ATS compatibility, identifies skill gaps, recommends relevant jobs and internships, generates interview preparation content, and provides personalized AI career guidance.
## Key Features
### Resume Analysis & ATS Scoring
* Upload PDF or DOCX resumes
* Automatic resume parsing and text extraction
* ATS score calculation with detailed breakdown
* Executive summary generation
* Strength and weakness identification
* Missing skills detection
### Smart Job & Internship Recommendations
* AI-powered role matching based on resume skills
* Personalized career recommendations
* Job and internship suggestions aligned with user profiles
* Automated LinkedIn job data extraction
* Salary, location, duration, and application link insights
### AI Resume Editor
* Section-wise resume editing
* ATS optimization suggestions
* Grammar and professional writing improvements
* Resume content rewriting
* Professional bullet-point generation
* Skill-gap analysis for target roles
### Interview Preparation
* Personalized interview questions
* Technical, Behavioral, HR, and Project-based rounds
* Suggested answers and preparation guidance
* Resume-specific interview practice
### AI Career Mentor
* Conversational AI assistant
* Personalized career guidance
* Resume-aware recommendations
* Learning roadmap suggestions
* Career growth insights
### PDF Report Generation
* Professional downloadable analysis reports
* ATS evaluation summary
* Skills assessment
* Career recommendations
---
## Workflow
1. User registers and authenticates securely.
2. Resume is uploaded in PDF/DOCX format.
3. Text is extracted and processed.
4. AI analyzes resume quality and ATS compatibility.
5. Job and internship recommendations are generated.
6. Analysis is stored for future access.
7. Interactive results are displayed on the dashboard.
8. Users can continue with Resume Editing, Interview Preparation, or AI Career Mentoring.
